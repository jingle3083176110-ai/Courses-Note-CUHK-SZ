#### 1. 计算技术的演进历程

#### 1. Evolution of Computing Technology

从 “机房级巨型机” 到 “掌上便携设备”，核心阶段及代表如下：

From "room-sized giants" to "palm-sized portable devices", the core stages and representatives are as follows:

  **Early Mainframe Computer**: ENIAC (1946, University of Pennsylvania), with 17,468 vacuum tubes, covering 170 m², weighing 30 tons, and capable of 5,000 operations per second—it was the first general-purpose electronic computer.

#### 2. 芯片（Microchip）核心知识

#### 2. Core Knowledge of Microchips

- **芯片制造**：附详细解析视频（https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1r9XzY9Eid/），需扫码参与相关问题作答。

- **摩尔定律（关键规律）**：

  **Moore’s Law (Key Principle)**:

  - 提出者：英特尔联合创始人 Gordon E. Moore（1965 年）；

    

    Proposer: Gordon E. Moore, co-founder of Intel (1965);

  - 核心内容：集成电路上的晶体管密度每 2 年翻倍，衍生出 “性能 / 容量每 2 年翻倍”“价格每 2 年减半” 的趋势；

    

    Core content: The transistor density on integrated circuits doubles every two years, leading to the trends of "doubling performance/capacity every two years" and "halving price every two years";

  - 当前现状：摩尔定律放缓 —— 晶体管密度翻倍周期超过 2 年，技术实现难度增加（投资回报率低），且 “安迪 - 比尔定律”（软件复杂度消耗芯片新增性能）进一步削弱实际体验提升。

    

    Current status: Moore’s Law is slowing down—the cycle for transistor density doubling exceeds two years, technology implementation becomes more difficult (low ROI), and "Andy and Bill’s Law" (software complexity consumes the new performance of chips) further weakens the improvement of actual user experience.

- **芯片应用领域（市场占比）**：

  **Microchip Application Fields (Market Share)**:

  - 汽车领域占比最高（34.50%），其次是计算领域（27.28%）、消费电子（12.94%）、工业（8.96%）、通信基础设施（7.82%）及其他（8.51%）。

    

    The automotive sector accounts for the highest proportion (34.50%), followed by computing (27.28%), consumer electronics (12.94%), industry (8.96%), communication infrastructure (7.82%), and others (8.51%).

- **芯片主要制造商（市场份额）**：

  **Major Microchip Manufacturers (Market Share)**:

  - 台积电（TSMC）占绝对主导（52.9%），其次是三星（17.3%）、联电（UMC，7.2%）、格芯（GlobalFoundries，6.1%）、中芯国际（SMIC，5.3%）等；

    

    Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) dominates (52.9%), followed by Samsung (17.3%), United Microelectronics Corporation (UMC, 7.2%), GlobalFoundries (6.1%), Semiconductor Manufacturing International Corporation (SMIC, 5.3%), etc.;

  - 注：5nm 芯片 80% 用于智能手机（如 A16 Bionic 芯片含 160 亿晶体管）。

    

    Note: 80% of 5nm chips are used in smartphones (e.g., the A16 Bionic chip contains 16 billion transistors).

- **芯片短缺问题**：

  **Microchip Shortage Issue**:

  - 短缺原因：①疫情 “居家令” 推动需求激增；②5G、物联网、AI、云计算等技术带动芯片需求上涨；③部分企业囤积芯片（存在 “芯片黄牛” 现象）；

    

    Causes of shortage: ① Surge in demand driven by COVID-19 "stay-at-home orders"; ② Rising demand for chips driven by technologies such as 5G, IoT, AI, and Cloud Computing; ③ Chip stockpiling by some enterprises (existence of "chip scalping");

  - 短缺影响：芯片作为新技术硬件基础，已成为贸易战中的 “关键武器”，中国芯片短缺还导致 AI 发展延迟。

    

    Impact of shortage: As the hardware foundation of new technologies, chips have become a "key weapon" in trade wars; the microchip shortage in China has also delayed AI development.

### （二）互联网与连接技术

### (2) Internet and Connectivity Technology

#### 1. 互联网与万维网（Web）的区别

#### 1. Difference Between the Internet and the Web

- 互联网（Internet）：全球互联的计算机网络，是 “基础设施”；

  

  Internet: A worldwide network of interconnected computers, serving as the "infrastructure";

- 万维网（Web）：通过互联网访问信息的方式，依赖 HTTP 协议，仅为互联网应用之一（其他应用包括邮件 SMTP、Skype VoIP 等）。

  

  Web: A way to access information via the Internet, relying on the HTTP protocol—it is only one of the Internet applications (other applications include email (SMTP), Skype (VoIP), etc.).

#### 2. 连接方式分类及特点

#### 2. Classification and Characteristics of Connectivity Methods

##### （1）有线通信

##### (1) Wired Communication

- 主要类型：双绞线、同轴电缆、光纤（由香港中文大学前校长高锟于 1965 年发明，2009 年获诺贝尔物理学奖）；

  

  Main types: Twisted-Pair Cable, Coaxial Cable, Optical Fiber (invented by Charles K. Kao, former President of The Chinese University of Hong Kong, in 1965; Nobel Prize in Physics in 2009);

- 传输速度对比：同轴电缆（2.3×10⁸ m/s）、光纤（2.0×10⁸ m/s）；

  

  Transmission speed comparison: Coaxial Cable (2.3×10⁸ m/s), Optical Fiber (2.0×10⁸ m/s);

- 信号衰减类比：同轴电缆如 “城市里的快车”（短距离有优势），光纤如 “高铁”（长距离更稳定）。

  

  Analogy for signal attenuation: Coaxial Cable is like a "fast car in the city" (advantageous for short distances), while Optical Fiber is like a "high-speed train" (more stable for long distances).

##### （2）无线通信

##### (2) Wireless Communication

- 主要类型：微波链路（如卫星）、GSM、WCDMA、WiFi、WiMax、3G/4G/5G、蓝牙、近场通信（NFC）；

  

  Main types: Microwave Links (e.g., satellites), GSM, WCDMA, WiFi, WiMax, 3G/4G/5G, Bluetooth, Near-Field Communication (NFC);

- 核心特点：依赖带宽与传输距离平衡（如 NFC 带宽低但距离近，5G 带宽高且覆盖广）。

  

  Core feature: Relies on a balance between bandwidth and transmission distance (e.g., NFC has low bandwidth but short distance, while 5G has high bandwidth and wide coverage).

##### （3）主流连接技术的优缺点

##### (3) Advantages and Disadvantages of Mainstream Connectivity Technologies

| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |

### （三）云计算（Cloud Computing）

### (3) Cloud Computing

#### 1. 云计算定义

#### 1. Definition of Cloud Computing

一种 “按需获取” 的计算机系统，计算资源（如高性能处理、存储）通常由**外部服务提供商**提供，支持机器学习、大数据等场景。

An "on-demand" computer system where computing resources (e.g., high-performance processing, storage) are usually provided by **external service providers**, supporting scenarios such as machine learning and big data.

#### 2. 传统计算与云计算的对比

#### 2. Comparison Between Traditional Computing and Cloud Computing

| 维度 (Dimension)     | 传统计算 (Traditional Computing)                             | 云计算 (Cloud Computing)                                     |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 架构 (Architecture)  | 本地服务器提供服务，数据 / 应用存储于本地设备                | 外部服务商提供资源，数据 / 应用存储于 “云端”                 |
|                      | Services provided by local servers; data/applications stored on local devices | Resources provided by external service providers; data/applications stored in the "cloud" |
| 优点 (Advantages)    | 可靠性高、操作响应快、敏感数据本地管控                       | 成本低（减少前期硬件投入）、可无限扩展、支持数据备份与灾难恢复 |
|                      | High reliability, fast operation response, local control of sensitive data | Low cost (reduces upfront hardware investment), infinitely scalable, supports data backup and disaster recovery |
| 缺点 (Disadvantages) | 硬件规格固定（生命周期内无法变更）、前期投入高、扩展性差     | 依赖互联网（断网无法使用）、存在隐私与安全风险               |
|                      | Fixed hardware specifications (unchangeable during lifecycle), high upfront cost, poor scalability | Relies on the Internet (unusable when offline), has privacy and security risks |

## 四、课程核心总结：IT 与 AI 的关联性

## 4. Core Course Summary: Interdependency Between IT and AI

IT 技术的发展是 AI 得以落地的 “基础设施、工具与场景基础”，二者高度依赖：

The development of IT technology serves as the "infrastructure, tools, and scenario foundation" for AI implementation, and the two are highly interdependent:

1. 数据层面

   ：互联网与传感器的普及催生 “数据爆炸”，为 AI 提供核心训练资源；

   Data Level

   : The popularization of the Internet and sensors has triggered a "data explosion", providing core training resources for AI;

2. 计算层面

   ：芯片（尤其是 GPU）、超算的性能提升，让大规模 AI 模型训练具备可行性；

   Computing Level

   : Improved performance of chips (especially GPUs) and supercomputers has made large-scale AI model training feasible;

3. 连接层面

   ：通信技术（5G）与云计算的发展，支持 AI 应用全球规模化部署。

   Connectivity Level

   : The development of communication technologies (5G) and Cloud Computing supports the global large-scale deployment of AI applications.
